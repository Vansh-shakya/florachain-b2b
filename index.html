Designed specifically around the commercial realities of plant logistics — perishability, transport damage, credit cycles, and agreed interest.

Key Capabilities

Built for Modern Nursery Commerce

Everything plant wholesalers and retail nurseries need to run smooth, transparent commercial trade.

🌿

Wholesale Plant Catalog

Manage inventory with botanical names, container specifications (pots, bags, root-balls), pricing tiers, and real-time nursery stock.

🚛

Fleet Dispatch & Manifest

Assign transport vehicles, record driver contacts, generate dispatch challans, and share live in-transit estimates with buyer nurseries.

✅

Physical Inspection Gate

Prevent invoice arguments: Nurseries inspect deliveries at the unloading dock, recording accepted sound counts and photographing damage.

💰

Verified-Only Invoicing

Invoices are mathematically generated only on sound delivered plants. Damaged or rejected plants are automatically credited or deducted.

⏳

Agreed Late-Payment Interest

Define custom credit terms (Net 15, Net 30, Net 45) and automatically compute contractual late interest (% p.a.) on overdue balances.

📊

Running Balance Ledger

Double-entry account statement for every nursery partner: Every invoice debits, every payment credits, providing complete audit transparency.
